# Forecast near-term Yellow Taxi demand

Build a daily history of published December 2025 Yellow Taxi trips by pickup
borough. Test a forecast model on the final week, then forecast the next seven
days. Check continuity and missing dates before fitting the model.

Explain the model, expected range, and major uncertainty. Present the result as
a demand forecast, not as a causal claim or a prediction of what a policy change
would produce.
